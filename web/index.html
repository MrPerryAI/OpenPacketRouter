Each row and column is one direction of one cable. A dot means "a frame on the row link becomes a frame on the column link one hop later." Magenta rows are links on a loop. During slow motion, lit rows are where frames are right now.

Teaching model, not a reconstruction. As of Nov 17, 2026 the FAA has not said what protocol carried the flood. Link delays and speeds are illustrative. The math (non-backtracking matrix spectral radius and a renewal equation for growth) was checked against an independent packet simulator in the OpenPacketRouter proof of concept.
